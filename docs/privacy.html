Privacy Policy

This page explains what data this site collects and how it's used. Last updated 2026.

Forms (contact, review)

The contact and review forms on this site send the information you type (name, email, company, and your message) to vFactor Solutions via Formspree, a third-party form-delivery service, so it can reach our inbox. We only use it to respond to you or, for reviews, to consider publishing what you submitted.

Resume ATS checker

The resume checker tool runs entirely in your browser. Your resume file is parsed locally and is never uploaded to any server. If you choose to click "Email this resume for open roles," it opens your own email app with a pre-filled message — nothing is sent automatically, and you choose whether to attach and send it.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and only records aggregate, anonymized visit counts.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other websites.
  • We don't store resume content anywhere — it stays in your browser session.
