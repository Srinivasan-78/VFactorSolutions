How we work

Five stages. One person accountable.

Every engagement runs the same way, so you always know what stage you're at and what lands next.

  1. 01

    Intake

    A working session on the role: the actual problem the hire solves, the success profile, the compensation band, and the constraints nobody writes into the JD.

    You get: a written role brief
  2. 02

    Sourcing

    Direct search across our network, referrals and databases — including passive candidates who are not applying to postings.

    You get: a live pipeline view
  3. 03

    Validation

    Every profile is spoken to, screened against the success profile, and checked on notice period, compensation expectation and genuine interest before it reaches you.

    You get: screened profiles only
  4. 04

    Shortlist

    A ranked shortlist with our reasoning on each candidate, plus interview scheduling and coordination handled on your behalf.

    You get: a ranked shortlist
  5. 05

    Offer and joining

    Offer negotiation, counter-offer management and follow-through until the candidate actually joins — the stage most agencies stop caring about.

    You get: joining follow-through

Engagement

Pick the model that fits the hire.

Three ways to work together. No lock-in on the first engagement, and no charge until the work does what it promised.

Contingency

Pay on joining

For individual open roles where you want reach without commitment. You pay only when a candidate we sourced joins.

  • Best for one-off or urgent roles
  • Fee as a percentage of annual CTC
  • Replacement guarantee period applies

Retained

Dedicated search

For leadership, confidential or hard-to-fill roles that need committed search time rather than opportunistic sourcing.

  • Best for leadership and niche skills
  • Staged fee across the search
  • Market mapping and search report included

RPO

Your recruitment function

For sustained hiring volume. We run sourcing and screening as an extension of your talent team, on a monthly engagement.

  • Best for continuous or volume hiring
  • Flat monthly engagement fee
  • Your ATS, your process, your branding

Not sure which applies? Describe the roles and we will tell you which model costs you least — including when that answer is "hire this one yourself".
